## 1. Executive Summary & Root Cause Investigation

When testing earlier builds with a single large continuous pothole, CivicLens AI exhibited two failure modes:
1. **Missed Large Potholes / False Uncertain Classification:**
   - Reports: `Potholes detected: 0`, `Primary damage: Other / uncertain`, `Model confidence: 85%`.
2. **Fragmented Duplicate Detections & False Positives on Background Objects:**
   - Multiple bounding boxes (e.g. 2 to 4 separate boxes) drawn across different parts of the **same physical crater**.
   - Background objects (cars, roadside trees, road dividers) occasionally triggered false-positive defect boxes.

## 3. Engineering Fixes Implemented

### Fix 1: Multi-Axis Crater Spatial Merging (`_merge_overlapping_potholes`)
Single physical potholes often feature surface aggregate bridges or uneven depths. We replaced the rigid 15 px gap check with an adaptive proximity & projection overlap test:
- **1D Projection Overlap:** Computes horizontal ($x$) and vertical ($y$) overlap ratios:
  $$\text{Overlap}_y = \frac{\max(0, \min(y_1 + h_1, y_2 + h_2) - \max(y_1, y_2))}{\min(h_1, h_2)}$$
- **Adaptive Fragment Radius:**
  $$\text{Allowed Gap} = \max\left(35\text{ px}, 0.35 \times \max(w_1, h_1, w_2, h_2)\right)$$
- If two boxes share $\ge 35\%$ projection overlap along one dimension and their gap along the perpendicular dimension is $\le \text{Allowed Gap}$, they are united into a single bounding box.
- Genuinely distinct potholes (separated by $\ge 60$ px) remain separate.

### Fix 2: Perspective & Pavement Enclosure Calibration
- Relaxed maximum candidate area limit from $35.0\%$ to $65.0\%$ of the frame to natively support close-up smartphone road photographs.
- Conditioned the horizon height check so that it only rejects candidates that lack upper pavement support (`(top_is_sky > 0.05 or top_is_veg > 0.05 or top_p < 0.20)`). Potholes located on the road surface with valid pavement enclosure are preserved.
- Adjusted minimum directional pavement enclosure to require either $\ge 35\%$ average boundary enclosure or at least 3 enclosing sides, allowing edge-of-road potholes near shoulders and curbs to pass.

### Fix 3: Water Puddle Reflectance Isolation
- Trapped standing water inside pothole cavities exhibits blue sky chromaticity (`b > r + 8` and `b > g + 4`) and flat internal surface texture ($\|\nabla I\| < 12.0$).
- Explicitly excluded `water_candidate` from `colored_vehicle_mask`, preventing water-filled potholes from being discarded as painted car bodies.
- Updated defect contrast scoring to $\text{contrast} = \max(0.15, |I_{\text{road}} - I_{\text{cavity}}| / I_{\text{road}})$, ensuring reflective puddles and dark hollows both produce valid confidence scores.

### Fix 4: Frontend UI Confidence Binding
- Updated [NewReportPage.tsx](file:///Users/ayushvinchurkar/Library/Mobile%20Documents/com~apple~CloudDocs/final%20year%20project/frontend/src/pages/NewReportPage.tsx#L995):
  ```tsx
  {aiInspectResult?.prediction_confidence !== undefined ? `${(aiInspectResult.prediction_confidence * 100).toFixed(0)}%` : '—'}
  ```
  Eliminated the hardcoded `85%` fallback. If no pothole is detected (`confidence = 0.0`), the UI accurately shows `0%` alongside the advisory prompt.
